## Problems & Pain Points

**Core problem:** After an accident, hail storm, or years of wear, vehicle owners need body repair that restores both the appearance and structural integrity of their car — but most shops cut corners, deliver inconsistent quality, or lack the skill for luxury, custom, and restoration work.

**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Chain shops and dealership body centers often prioritize volume over quality; results feel generic
- Many independents lack computerized color matching, advanced structural repair equipment, or training for exotic materials
- Poor communication — customers feel out of the loop about timelines and costs
- "Budget" shops may use aftermarket or substandard parts, compromising safety and finish
- Few shops in the area handle both insurance collision repair AND custom/restoration work under one roof

**What it costs them:** Visible paint mismatch, recurring rattles or alignment issues, depreciated resale value, and the anxiety of not knowing if the repair was truly done right.

**Emotional tension:** Fear of being overcharged or misled; stress of navigating insurance; frustration when a "finished" car still doesn't look right; worry about safety after structural damage.

## Competitive Landscape

**Direct competitors:** Other independent auto body shops in Garland, Mesquite, Richardson, and greater DFW — typically smaller operations that may lack the equipment, range of services, or 5-star review volume that Texas Five Star has built.

**Secondary competitors:** Dealership body shops — factory-affiliated but often more expensive, longer wait times, and less personal attention. Service Kinging / Caliber Collision type chains — fast turnaround but assembly-line quality.

**Indirect competitors:** DIY touch-up kits, mobile dent repair services, or simply ignoring the damage.

**How each falls short for customers:** Chains and dealerships lack the personal, family-owned touch and artisan-grade custom work. DIY and mobile services can't handle structural repair, full repaints, or restorations.

## Design Aesthetic

> **CRITICAL: This project uses a high-end, Apple-like glassmorphism design aesthetic.**

**Visual identity:**
- **Color palette:** Deep neutral-950 (#0a0a0a) backgrounds, red-600 (#dc2626) as the primary accent, white/gray text hierarchy (white → gray-200 → gray-300 → gray-400 → gray-500)
- **Design system:** Dark-mode-first, cinematic, editorial. Full-bleed hero imagery with gradient overlays (from-black/80 via-black/50 to-transparent). Dramatic typography with tight tracking.
- **Glassmorphism elements:** `backdrop-blur-sm`, `backdrop-blur-xl`, `bg-white/5`, `bg-black/20`, `border border-white/10`, `border-white/20` — used on testimonial cards, mobile nav overlay, slider controls, and CTA buttons
- **Typography:** Geist Sans + Geist Mono (Google Fonts). Font-black for headlines, font-bold for subheads, font-semibold for nav/brand. Tracking-tighter on all major headings.
- **Motion & animation:** Framer Motion for scroll-reveal (fadeUp), staggered star animations, infinite vertical marquee testimonials, Ken Burns effect on hero backgrounds. Hover transforms (scale-105, scale-110) on cards and images. 1000ms crossfade on hero carousel.
- **Layout:** Max-width 85rem container. Generous whitespace (py-16 to py-32 sections). 2-column and 3-column grids. Rounded-2xl cards with shadow-2xl.
- **Interactive elements:** Before/after image comparison sliders with draggable red handle, lightbox modal with backdrop-blur-lg, slide dot pagination, mobile menu with translate-x animation
- **Icon system:** Feather Icons (react-icons/fi) for UI, Font Awesome (react-icons/fa) for social/brand icons

**Design principles:**
1. Every surface should feel premium — no plain white backgrounds, no generic styling
2. Photography is hero-level quality, full-bleed, with cinematic overlays
3. Red-600 is used sparingly for maximum impact: CTAs, accent bars, icon backgrounds, active states
4. Glassmorphism should feel subtle and sophisticated, never gimmicky
5. Motion should be purposeful — enhancing the perception of quality, not distracting

# Pricing & Insurance Information — Texas Five Star Paint & Body

At Texas Five Star Paint & Body, we offer premium, dealership-quality work at competitive, transparent prices. All of our pricing is strictly per-job because every vehicle and damage situation is unique.

## Insurance & Claims
- **All Major Insurance Accepted:** We work directly with all major insurance companies.
- **Claims Assistance:** We handle all the paperwork, negotiation, and communication with the insurance adjuster.
- **Deductible Forgiveness:** We offer up to $500 deductible forgiveness on qualifying insurance collision repairs. (Contact us for eligibility criteria).

## Free Estimates
- **Cost:** $0
- **Process:** Bring your vehicle in or send us detailed photos. We provide a comprehensive, itemized estimate outlining parts, labor, and materials. No hidden fees.

## Typical Service Starting Points
*(Note: These are baseline estimates for typical jobs. Final pricing requires physical inspection.)*

### Paintless Dent Repair (PDR)
- **Small Dings (Door dings, etc.):** Typically starts at $75 - $150 per dent.
- **Hail Damage:** Requires full assessment. We work with your comprehensive insurance coverage.

### Collision Repair
- **Minor Bumper Repair / Scratch Removal:** Starts around $300 - $600.
- **Major Structural / Panel Replacement:** Requires an estimate. Handled predominantly via insurance claims.

### Custom Paint & Refinishing
- **Full Vehicle Repaint (Standard):** Starts at $3,500+.
- **Custom Finishes (Candy, Pearl, Matte):** Varies significantly based on materials, prep work, and color shifting required. Starts at $6,000+.

### Classic Auto Restoration
- **Frame-Off / Rotisserie Restoration:** Highly custom, often billed progressively or at an agreed milestone rate. Expect significant investment for concourse-level factory specifications.

### Minor Reconditioning
- **Headlight Restoration:** $50 - $100 per pair.
- **Lease Turn-In Prep:** Varies based on the inspection. Saves customers an average of $300 - $800 compared to dealership turn-in penalties.
